Pre-Call Engagement Flow

Black — the message you send Blue — when to send it, never sent

Purpose: The point of pre-call engagement is to get the prospect to:

  • Reply and have a basic conversation with us before the call
  • Engage with content that's relevant to what their intention is with the call.

Most people book with a personal email, so the first message is simply the opener for that conversation.

Step 1 — CRM check

  1. Open the prospect's record in the CRM.
  2. Identify their segment:
    • Business owner
    • Marketer / agency
    • Nine-to-fiver / corporate professional
  3. Check whether they signed up with a personal email or a business email.
    • The vast majority sign up with personal emails.

Answer these as the conversation goes. Every message you need comes back below in the order you send them.
